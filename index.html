About

From mathematical ideas to systems that actually run.

I am a research engineer with a Ph.D. in Electronics Engineering and a background spanning wireless communications, signal processing, optimization, machine learning and software development.

At Gatehouse Satcom, I develop high-performance C++ algorithms for 5G NR satellite communication systems, including synchronization, channel estimation, equalization, timing compensation and link-level analysis.

I especially enjoy problems where theory meets implementation: a clean mathematical model is only the beginning — the real challenge is making it robust, testable and useful in a real engineering system.

Technical focus

The problems I like to work on.

My work sits at the intersection of wireless systems, data-driven methods and implementation.

01 · Physical layer

5G NR & NTN

Baseband processing, synchronization, channel estimation, equalization, timing compensation, radio measurements and implementation of standards-driven algorithms for satellite-enabled 5G.

02 · Satellite systems

Satellite Communications

Resource allocation, GEO/MEO/LEO scenarios, Doppler and delay challenges, Earth observation and communication-efficient satellite applications.

03 · Intelligent systems

Machine Learning & Data

Time-series modeling, anomaly detection, neural networks, reinforcement learning, federated learning, statistical evaluation and data-driven decision making for engineering systems.

04 · Cyber-physical systems

Digital Twins & ISAC

State estimation, value of information, networked control, integrated sensing and communications, cloud-based digital twins and latency-aware decision making.
