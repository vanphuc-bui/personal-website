About

Engineering wireless systems from theory to implementation.

I am a wireless communications engineer and researcher with a background in physical-layer design, signal processing and next-generation mobile communication systems.

My work spans 5G NR PHY, non-terrestrial networks, satellite communications, channel and signal processing, as well as machine-learning-driven methods for communication and control systems.

I enjoy problems that sit between research and implementation: mathematically interesting enough to require deep reasoning, but concrete enough to end up running in a real system.
